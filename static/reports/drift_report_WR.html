Statistical Drift Criteria

Continuous Physical Metrics (demand_mw, weather, rolling std)
Flagged when normalized 1D Wasserstein distance W₁ / σ ≥ 0.50 and two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov p < 0.01.
