System Architecture & Specifications

1. Ingestion Cron Job: GitHub Actions scheduled cron runs every 6 hours (15 */6 * * *), ingesting POSOCO / NLDC demand telemetry and Open-Meteo meteorological observations with Pydantic v2 validation.
2. Zero-Leakage Feature Pipeline: Vectorized Polars LazyFrames computing autoregressive lags, rolling statistics, and continuous Fourier harmonics with a mandatory shift(1) causality fence.
3. Dual Model Engine: LightGBM multi-step quantile regression (p10, p50, p90) with isotonic monotonicity sorting coupled with Scikit-Learn Isolation Forest for structural anomaly detection.
4. Explainability & Observability: SHAP TreeExplainer with 150 k-means background centroids extracting top-3 physical drivers, and Evidently AI performing KS and Wasserstein drift evaluation.
